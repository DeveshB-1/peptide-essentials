Peptide Essentials – Privacy Policy

Effective 13 October 2026

Peptide Essentials ("the app") is an offline Android app, developed by DeveshB-1, for keeping a personal log of medication you have been prescribed. This policy explains what happens to the information you enter.

Data we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. The developer receives no data from the app and has no way to see what you enter.

No network access

The app does not request the Android INTERNET permission, so it cannot send or receive anything over the network.

Data stored on your device

The medications, schedules, vials, dose history, injection sites, measurements, journal entries and notes you enter are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. They are excluded from Android cloud backup. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes them.

Export and backup

You can create a PDF report, export CSV files or save a backup file. Both happen only when you choose to, and you decide where the file goes (for example a folder on your phone, or an app you pick from the share sheet). Once a file leaves the app, it is handled by whatever app or service you sent it to.

Permissions

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) – shows a reminder at each scheduled dose time, with a "Mark taken" button. You can refuse or turn it off at any time; the app still works without reminders.
  • Run at startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED) – Android clears scheduled reminders when the phone restarts, so the app re-schedules them after a reboot. It also re-schedules them when the time or time zone changes.

The app does not request storage, location, contacts, camera or any other permissions.

Not medical advice

Peptide Essentials is a personal log, not a medical device. It does not recommend medications, doses or injection sites. Always follow the instructions of your prescriber or pharmacist.

Children

The app is intended for adults and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
