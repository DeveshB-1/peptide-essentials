Peptide Essentials is an all-in-one, private tracker for the peptide and injection medications you have been prescribed — from mixing a vial to logging every dose, rotating injection sites and seeing how you're progressing.

You add your own medications and enter everything exactly as your prescriber or pharmacist told you. The app keeps it all organised, reminds you on time, and gives you a clear record to share with your care team.

TODAY AT A GLANCE
• Doses due today, any you missed, and what's coming up
• "Draw N units" shown right on each dose, worked out from your active vial
• Log a dose as Taken or Skipped in a tap — even straight from the reminder

VIAL INVENTORY
• Track every vial: amount, water added, mixing date, use-by date and lot
• Remaining amount counts down automatically with each dose
• Alerts when a vial is running low or about to expire

BUILT-IN CALCULATOR
• Reconstitution maths: concentration, units to draw and doses per vial
• To-scale U-100 syringe guide for 0.3, 0.5 and 1 mL syringes
• Reverse mode: see what dose a number of units contains
• Clear warnings for draws that won't fit or are too small to measure

INJECTION SITES
• Tappable front and back body model with 10 sites (abdomen upper & lower, thighs, upper arms, glutes — left and right)
• Heat map shows which sites were used recently and which have rested
• Suggests the best site to use next, respecting a rest period you set
• Per-site history, including any reactions you noted

PROGRESS
• Weight, waist, body fat and your own custom measurements
• Trend charts (1M / 3M / 6M / All) with dose days marked
• Change since you started

JOURNAL & TIMELINE
• Daily ratings for energy, sleep, appetite and mood
• Side effects you can tap in, plus your own
• A day-by-day timeline of doses, vials, measurements and notes

REPORTS & BACKUP
• PDF report for your prescriber: adherence, doses, trends and side effects
• CSV export of every table
• Full backup and restore to move to a new phone

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No accounts, no ads, no tracking. The app has no internet access — everything stays on your phone.

IMPORTANT
Peptide Essentials is a personal log, not medical advice or a medical device. It does not recommend medications, doses or schedules. Always follow your prescriber's or pharmacist's instructions.
